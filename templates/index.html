About VoiceCraft AI

VoiceCraft AI is a text-to-speech application powered by the world-class ElevenLabs API. It provides an intuitive interface for generating realistic human speech for various applications including educational narration, video voiceovers, and storytelling.

Note: Voice availability, language support, and generation limits depend on the configured ElevenLabs account and selected model.
